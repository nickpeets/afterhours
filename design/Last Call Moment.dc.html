Ruling · under 20 seconds left
Lock the button at T-20, and offer to queue it. Truncating sells a twenty-second product and delivers six — for a paid feature that's a refund conversation, and it teaches people the thing is unreliable. Letting it run past the phase boundary is worse: a purchase would be overriding the show's clock, which breaks the one rule everything else here rests on.
So at 0:20 remaining the control greys with NOT ENOUGH FLOOR LEFT, and offers one tap to arm it for the next open floor — it fires automatically the second that phase opens, before anyone else can. He loses nothing, the show keeps its clock, and the pressure to use it early is a feature.
Round 3's open floor is the exception: it's 90s of deliberation, so the lock lands at T-20 there too and there is no next phase to queue into. Arm it earlier or lose it.
Three things to settle
This replaces the floor hold. The spec already has a paid 15s mute at 10 ✦, mingle-only. Two overlapping paid mutes in one phase is one too many — retire the old one and let the moment be the mechanic.
It isn't private, and the copy shouldn't say it is. Dev and Sam are muted only on Marla's feed and June's — the two of them can still hear and see each other, and the rest of the room never loses a thing. "Private moment" promises a booth we're not building; the floor is taken is true from every seat.
Both mutes are real, and independent. Marla's updateReceiveSettings silences Dev and Sam on her end; June's does the same on his — two separate calls, so one can land without the other. If either fails to apply in time, drop the moment and refund rather than run it half-blocked.
