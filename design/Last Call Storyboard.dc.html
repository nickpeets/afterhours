TURN 6 · ONE EPISODE, THREE POINTS OF VIEW · 6a
THE STORYBOARD
Six beats down, three columns across. Same room, same second — what changes is what you're allowed to see and what you're allowed to do. The line is dark for everyone until a chair opens; then it's a fifteen-second storm. Read a row to compare; read a column to live one night as one person.
HOST · SHE RUNS IT
Sees the chairs in full, the line only as a number. Asks, skips, decides once at the end.
CHAIR · HE'S AUDITIONING
Sees her and his rivals. Can't see the queue behind him — only how deep it is.
ROOM · THEY CAST IT
Sees her and the chairs. The line is dark during play — but when a chair opens, the crowd's hearts pick who fills it.
